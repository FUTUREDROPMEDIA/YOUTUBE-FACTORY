Privacy Policy — YouTube Factory

Last updated: November 23, 2026

YouTube Factory is a private tool operated by its owner for his own accounts only. There are no other users.

What data it accesses

  • With the owner's permission (OAuth), it uploads videos the owner approved to his own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
  • It reads analytics of those same accounts (views, average view percentage, watch time, shares, saves).

Where data is stored

Access tokens and analytics are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by him. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Google user data

YouTube Factory's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions, in Instagram's "Apps and websites" settings, or in TikTok's "Manage app permissions". Deleting the local files removes all stored data.
