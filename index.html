YouTube Factory

YouTube Factory is a private tool used by one creator to publish his own videos to his own channels (FutureDrop, BuildDrop and ViceDrop on YouTube, his Instagram accounts and his TikTok account) after he approves each one, and to read those channels' own analytics to decide what to make next.

It has no other users. It does not collect data from anyone else's accounts.
